# Mug: product one-pager

**What it is:** Catalogs character and sculpted mugs from brand shops, and gives collectors a shelf to track, share and compare

**Who it is for:** a collector of character mugs (3D heads, shaped bodies, tiki, teapots) who has more of them than they can keep in their head, wants to know which exist before buying a duplicate or missing a release, and would like to show the shelf to other collectors; and the one person who keeps the catalogue honest, working through a review queue.

**Register:** product (a tool someone uses), not brand (a page someone visits).

**The scene:** evening, on a phone or laptop, next to the actual shelf of mugs: checking whether the Pikachu head they just saw is the 475 ml ABYstyle one they already own, marking it wanted, and scrolling what other collectors added this week. Photos of glossy ceramic on white shop backgrounds, shown on a dark page, so every product image stands on its own warm porcelain tile.

**What done looks like:** the collector knows in one search whether a mug exists, what it holds and who makes it, and has it on their shelf in one tap; the admin ends a session with an empty review queue.
